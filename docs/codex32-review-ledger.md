## Findings and limits

- **Fixed:** The no-padding 160-bit constructor accepted an impossible padding value (`fbe72467`).
- **Fixed in tests:** The share-export fixture checked for a detached `.sig` file after its verifier had already validated and deleted that file. Corrected full Codex32 simulator suites passed on all three models at the documented evidence revision.
- **Fixed in tests and infrastructure:** Q1 QR seed import, Seed Vault menu entry, the blocked signing screen, and Codex32 screen transitions needed bounded readiness waits. Codex32/XPRV signing comparisons explicitly select Testnet4; the transaction-input explorer sets and restores the warning policy it asserts. The Bitcoin Core fixture starts an isolated daemon with bounded startup and process-group cleanup. Earlier diagnostic failures remain in the artifact tree and are not counted as qualified runs.
- **Regression evidence:** Full corrected Codex32 UI suites passed on Mk4, Mk5 and Q1; three independent save/restart/discard repetitions passed on all three. Host conformance passed 385 tests, and pinned Python/Rust comparisons had zero disagreements within their classified capabilities. Mk5 Seed XOR and UX suites passed with exact Q1-only skip ledgers and Q1 counterpart suites; Mk4 passphrases and Q1 teleport also passed. A local Bitcoin Core 32.0rc2 daemon enabled 34 Q1 Core-dependent cases. The dated artifact manifests supply exact case IDs and counts.
- **Fixed by integrated PRs:** CW1/MS1 checksum length ambiguity (#62/#64), blank-master pending-share storage (#65), saved-share validation and recovery ordering (#66/#63), 256-bit dice-only threshold (#67), unrelated export signatures (#68), and CW1/CX1 recovery explanation (#69).
- **Intentional:** Recovery remains unverified until the user compares a known address with the original network, path and address type. A checksum and short fingerprint do not authenticate the original wallet.
- **Hardware limit:** The simulator does not establish secure-element behavior, flash erasure atomicity, power-loss recovery or physical RNG quality. The documented interval between clearing saved progress and completing activation still exists.
- **Release status:** `artifacts/codex32/2026-10-04-integration/qualification-report.json` records final source-revision gates, artifacts, and remaining limitations. The listed historical regression results alone do not qualify a tagged release.
